01 // characters

Characters

The Breakers, the staff and the enemies of NeoArcadia-9.

B-Rank · Breaker

Aria Bloom

Bubbly implant fanatic who insists she is the Arcadia Queen, a vampire. Wields Vampyre, a giant fanged axe, and idolizes Kessler.

A-Rank · Nexuscore heiress

Cassia Vael

Poised, elitist and hungry for earned respect. Her rapier Regent hacks and disables tech. Sibling of instructor Gideon Rourke.

A-Rank · Street fighter

Rhea Terui

Laid-back, blue-skinned duelist who lives for rematches. Her One-Horned gloves launch cable-hands and shockwaves. Teases Noah, all in good fun.

B-Rank · Heavy gunner

Vex Moreau

Loud, bossy and cheerful, with a cyber-shark tail. Her shoulder cannon Battleship fires three explosive shots. Close with Mika.

C-Rank · Breaker

Theo Wren

Kind, stubborn and determined, with minimal chrome. His telekinetic shield Bastion pulls and pushes, and he watches over Noah.

D-Rank · Outcast

Noah Ferris

Reclusive, unchromed and the weakest student. Her replicating drones, Hollow, protect her with electric blasts.

A-Rank · Instructor

Helena Marsh

Ex-Nexuscore soldier, exhausted and cynical, secretly caring. Warns every student against too much chrome, and is friends with almost all of them.

A-Rank · Nexuscore instructor

Gideon Rourke

Harsh, scarred field soldier in heavy combat chrome. Teaches several subjects and drills students like recruits.

C-Rank · Academy mechanic

Mika Sorrel

Warm, deadpan and meticulous. Installs your implants, gore jokes included, and never harms a patient. Close friend of Vex.

S-Rank · Retired mercenary

Viktor Hale

"Black Zarathustra", a NeoArcadia legend. Stoic and laconic, with a heavy revolver and no use for borrowed chrome. Kessler made him retire.

AS-Rank · NOISE executor

Kessler

Eerily calm, rule-obsessed sadist who apologizes before violence. Wields Sun, flesh-piercing spiked chains wreathed in artificial flame.

A-Rank · NOISE enforcer

Kira

A broken cortex swaps her between a giggly, violent red self and a silent, precise blue one. Fights to the beat of her implant music.

S-Rank · NOISE leader

Null

Silent,unreadable and obsessed with antimatter, Null hides her motives behind chrome and a mask. She secretly funded your scholarship after recognizing your rare tolerance, eager to see what kind of monster your future becomes.

03 // the world

The World

The essentials, nothing more.

Nexuscore

A tech megacorp that fights NOISE because overchroming fuels psychosis, crime and addiction. It runs Ascendant Academy.

NOISE

Implant supremacists led by Auden Null. They despise the unchromed and force chrome on civilians. "Flesh is messy noise; technology is the new signal."

Breakers

Ascendant's students, trained to fight back with chrome and tech. They also work as Nexuscore rookies on real missions.

Cyber-Gear

Every student carries a cube that becomes a unique, extremely powerful signature weapon.

Implants & Humanity

Academy winners earn Cybernetic Tokens, redeemable for implants. Every implant costs a little humanity, and street chrome costs more.

Power Ranks

AS > S > A > B > C > D. A one-rank gap can be bridged by strategy. Two or more is nearly insurmountable.
